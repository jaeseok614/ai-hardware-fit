## English post

### Title

> Show HN: Pick a GPU and get a three-model local AI shortlist

### Body

> I maintain AI Hardware Fit, an open-source browser calculator for local AI hardware. This release focuses on the screen after GPU selection: it starts with three model candidates and compares VRAM fit, quantization, and estimated speed without loading the heavier comparison and benchmark workspaces.
>
> Here is a direct RTX 5070 Ti 16GB example:
> https://jaeseok614.github.io/ai-hardware-fit/?lang=en&gpu=rtx5070ti-16
>
> No account or install is required, inputs stay in the browser, and the formulas and source data are public. Speed is a planning estimate rather than a benchmark guarantee. Real-run corrections are especially welcome.

## 게시 전 확인

- 링크가 lang과 gpu만 포함하는 짧은 주소인지 확인합니다.
- 데스크톱과 모바일 결과 화면을 각각 한 번 엽니다.
- “benchmark”나 “guaranteed” 대신 “planning estimate”라고 씁니다.

- 실제 제보가 오면 먼저 출처와 조건을 확인한 뒤 반영합니다.
