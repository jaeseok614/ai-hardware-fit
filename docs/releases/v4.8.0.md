# v4.8.0 — AI Infrastructure Sizing

AI Hardware Fit v4.8 turns model, traffic, latency, availability, and budget assumptions into three reviewable infrastructure options. This release completes the workflow from quick sizing through editable BOM validation, commercial pricing, rack/network/power topology, approval, and proposal comparison.

## Highlights

- **Three options:** compare economy, recommended, and scalable plans by proposal price, SLA, capacity, failover, and evidence confidence.
- **Validated BOM:** check CPU/socket fit, RAM and storage capacity, NIC requirements, PSU/UPS headroom, and GPU chassis fit.
- **Commercial pricing:** apply supplier quote metadata, discount, margin, VAT, price date, and validity period.
- **Infrastructure topology:** estimate rack units, switches, NIC links, optics, cables, redundant PDU circuits, and cooling demand.
- **Quote workflow:** move an estimate through draft, review, approval, and revision states with reviewer and approver records.
- **Editable outputs:** export Excel worksheets for the BOM, commercial quote, topology, validation, and approval history.
- **Shareable examples:** open RAG, chatbot, and avatar-chat scenarios directly with a user count.
