## Mistral Large 4 1T A52B

- [Publisher card](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B): approximately 1T total parameters, 52B active including embeddings/output, 1,048,576-token context; native FP8 checkpoint, vision and DSpark draft layers. No speculative acceleration is assumed.
- [Publisher-linked GGUF](https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B-GGUF) and [file metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/ggml-org/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B-GGUF?blobs=true), checked 2026-11-19. Weight sizing sums every shard plus the 3,444,696,416-byte vision projector and adds the existing 8% planning overhead. It does not size weights from active parameters.
- Shard bytes: IQ2_XXS 297676048832; Q2_K 380706433472; Q4_K_M 634802244608; Q8_0 1115150340608; BF16 2098861021920. BF16 GGUF is an upcast of the original FP8 checkpoint.
- Only published GGUF formats receive launch commands. Current compatible llama.cpp is required; MLX support is unverified. KV sizing remains a generic planning estimate, not an architecture-exact MLA/shared-cache implementation.
- AA Intelligence Index v5: 26, publisher report, not a local throughput measurement. Speed and first-token latency are withheld pending calibration.
- [License](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B/blob/main/LICENSE): Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight; MaaS operators and affiliates need to review its commercial-use restrictions. Conditional commercial-use label retained.

## LiquidAI/LFM3-VL-2B

- [Publisher card](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM3-VL-2B), [config](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM3-VL-2B/raw/main/config.json), [checkpoint metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/LiquidAI/LFM3-VL-2B), checked 2026-11-19.
- 1,918,181,888 total BF16 parameters, including vision/projector; 24 decoder layers, 4 KV heads, head dimension 128, 16-pixel image patches with 2× merge. The catalog estimates single-image BF16 only.
- Hybrid cache has 13 convolution, 5 sliding and 6 full-attention layers. The vision estimator conservatively applies all 24 decoder layers to KV sizing. Its 8192 image-token and 4096 output-token settings are planning caps, not claims about the official 128000-token context. Video/multiframe memory is not modeled.
- Authored publisher recipe requires Transformers >=5.21.0. vLLM is in progress and SGLang is in review; autogenerated Hub snippets are not treated as verified support. The supplied command uses BF16 Transformers and caps generation at 512 tokens.
- [Publisher evaluation](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM3-VL-2B/raw/main/.eval_results/LFM3-VL-2B.yaml): MMMU-Pro standard, 10 options, 45.7. No matched hardware throughput baseline; page speed is withheld.
- LFM Open License v1.0 uses the existing Liquid AI conditional license policy.
